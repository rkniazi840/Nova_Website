Who We Are

Building a Sustainable Future Together

NOVA Environmental Consulting is a Qatar-based firm dedicated to advancing environmental stewardship across industries. We operate in full compliance with Qatar's environmental legislation — including Law No. 30 of 2002 and its amendments — and align our work with the environmental and human development pillars of Qatar National Vision 2030 (QNV 2030).

Our services also support organisations in meeting the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (UN SDGs), particularly SDGs 11, 13, 15, and 17. We are committed to a cleaner, safer, and more sustainable Qatar.
