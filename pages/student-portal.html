Getting Started

  1. Receive your account. After enrolment, your username and a temporary password are emailed to you.
  2. Complete the induction. Your first course contains the Student Handbook, academic calendar, and assessment policies.
  3. Open your course. All enrolled courses appear on your dashboard after login.
  4. Work through each unit. Study the learning materials, then complete the assignment for that unit.
  5. Submit and review feedback. Upload your work before the deadline and check your tutor's feedback once marking is complete.

Technical Requirements

Any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari) on a desktop, tablet or mobile device. A stable internet connection is recommended for uploading assignments and viewing recorded sessions.

Inside the Platform

Everything You Need in One Place

The learning platform supports every stage of your qualification, from first lesson to final result.

Teaching Materials

Each unit contains a Learning Materials section with lecture slides, reading lists, guidance notes, and recorded sessions, organised unit by unit.

Assignment Briefs

Every unit publishes its assignment brief alongside the learning materials, setting out the tasks, assessment criteria, word count, and submission deadline.

Assignment Submission

Completed work is uploaded directly to the unit's submission area. The platform records your submission time and confirms receipt by email.

Tutor Feedback

Once marking is complete, your tutor's written feedback, grade, and completed assessment grid appear against your submission for you to review.

Progress Tracking

A progress bar on each course shows which activities you have completed, and your personal grade report lists every result achieved so far.

Announcements & Support

Course announcements, the Student Handbook, academic calendar, assessment and plagiarism policies, and learner support contacts are always available.
