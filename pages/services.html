Our Services

We offer an integrated suite of environmental consulting services tailored to Qatar's regulatory environment, climatic conditions, and national development priorities. Each service helps clients achieve compliance, sustainability, and operational excellence.

Planning & Legal Compliance

Navigating Qatar's environmental regulatory framework

We guide organisations through Qatar's environmental regulatory framework, ensuring full compliance with applicable laws and standards. Our planning services help clients navigate Environmental Impact Assessments (EIA) and obtain the necessary environmental permits, in line with requirements set by the Ministry of Environment and Climate Change (MECC), MMUP, and Ashghal.

  • Environmental Management Planning (EMP)
  • Environmental Management System (EMS)
  • Environmental & Social Impact Assessment (ESIA)
  • Hospital Environment Management Consultation
  • Gas Chromatograph Support

Training & Consultation

Internationally accredited training adapted to Qatar's regulations

We deliver internationally accredited training programmes and professional development courses adapted to Qatar's workplace regulations and Gulf operational conditions. Our training empowers teams to manage environmental and health & safety challenges with confidence, competence, and full regulatory awareness.

  • ISO 14001:2015 Environmental Management Systems Training
  • ISO 45001 — Occupational Health & Safety
  • ISEP / IEMA Consultation
  • BREEAM — Building Research Establishment Environmental Assessment
  • LEED — Leadership in Energy and Environmental Design
  • Ergonomics Training
  • Environmental Law & Legal Compliance
  • NEBOSH & IOSH Safety Consultation
  • Safety Management & Workplace Safety Training
  • Laboratory Safety & Correct Procedures
  • Leadership & Management Skills for Sustainable Goals

Carbon Footprint & GHG Audit

Supporting Qatar's National Climate Action Plan and Paris Agreement commitments

As Qatar advances its National Climate Action Plan and its commitments under the Paris Agreement, understanding and managing greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions has become essential. We conduct thorough gap analyses and GHG audits to measure your organisation's carbon footprint and identify practical pathways toward net-zero, in alignment with Qatar's national decarbonisation strategy.

  • GHG Emissions Inventory & Verification
  • Carbon Footprint Assessment & Reporting
  • Audit Gap Analysis
  • Net-Zero Pathway Strategy

Health & Safety

Occupational health and safety in accordance with Qatar Labour Law

Protecting people is at the core of sustainable development. We provide comprehensive occupational health and safety (OHS) services in accordance with Qatar Labour Law and the guidelines of Qatar's Ministry of Labour. Our programmes are specifically designed for Qatar's demanding climatic conditions, including heat stress management protocols for outdoor workers.

  • Noise Impact Assessment
  • Air Quality & Dispersion Modelling
  • Ergonomics Assessment
  • Safety Management & Workplace Safety Training
  • ESG Strategy & Reporting

Green Building Consultation

GSAS, LEED, and BREEAM certification for Qatar's construction sector

Qatar's construction sector is one of the most active in the region. We support developers, architects, and contractors in achieving internationally recognised green building certifications — including Qatar's own Global Sustainability Assessment System (GSAS) and LEED. Our consultants are experienced in Qatar's built environment, climate challenges, and regulatory requirements under Ashghal and the Ministry of Municipality.

  • GSAS Certification Support
  • BREEAM — Building Research Establishment Environmental Assessment
  • LEED — Leadership in Energy and Environmental Design
  • Green Infrastructure Design Advice

Laboratory & Equipment

Precise environmental testing compliant with MECC requirements

Reliable data is the foundation of sound environmental decision-making. Our laboratory services provide precise testing and measurement of key environmental indicators — from air and water quality to soil contamination and noise levels — in compliance with international analytical standards and Qatar's environmental monitoring requirements as set by the Ministry of Environment and Climate Change (MECC).

  • Testing & Measurement of Environmental Indicators
  • Air Quality Analysis
  • Water Quality & Wastewater Testing
  • Soil & Contamination Analysis
  • Noise Measurement
  • Laboratory Information Management System (LIMS)

Renewability & Recycling Consultation

Circular economy strategies and sustainable resource management

As Qatar moves toward a circular economy model in line with QNV 2030, effective waste reduction and resource recovery are essential. We help organisations develop and implement renewability and recycling strategies that minimise waste, maximise resource efficiency, and support Qatar's national sustainability targets.

  • Waste Minimisation & Recycling Strategy
  • Circular Economy Advisory
  • Renewable Materials & Resource Recovery
  • Sustainable Procurement & Supply Chain Guidance
